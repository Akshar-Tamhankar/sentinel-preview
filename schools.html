Student data and privacy

What the app stores and what it doesn't do.

What the app stores about each student

Every field below is used for check-in, points or prizes. The app stores nothing else about students.

These are the fields the app stores about a student today.
Field What it holds Why it's needed
Name The student's name So staff know they have the right student, and can find a student by name if a QR code won't scan.
Email The student's email address So the student can sign in. With the new sign-in, it also gets the one-time sign-in code.
Student ID number The ID number your school issued So staff can match the account to a student at your school, and look a student up at the gate.
Membership A random membership code (what the QR code holds), whether the membership is active, and when it expires So a scan finds the right account without the student's name in the QR code, and only active members earn points.
Attendance Which game, what time, which staff member checked the student in, and whether by QR code or by hand So a student earns points once per game, and every check-in can be traced to the person who made it.
Points history Every award, purchase, refund and correction, with who made it and the reason So any balance can be checked line by line, and no one can change points without a record.
Prize reservations Which prize the student reserved, and whether it's been picked up So no prize is promised twice, and points go back if a pickup is canceled.

The app doesn't ask for a home address, phone number, birth date or grades. When the new sign-in goes live, each account will also have a password.

What the app doesn't do

  • No personal details in the QR codeThe QR code holds only a random code. It isn't a password, and it doesn't hold a name or a points balance.
  • No ads, analytics or outside scriptsThe app loads no ad code, analytics or scripts from other companies. Its scanner and QR code software are served from the app's own server.
  • No link to your ticketingThe app doesn't connect to GoFan or any ticketing system. Staff confirm admission the way they do now.
  • No app to install, no hardware to buyGate workers use the camera in a web browser on their phone.

Who can see and change what

The school sets every role
Each person is a student, gate worker, admin or head admin. Roles are set on the server by the school, so no one can pick their own.
Some powers belong to the head admin only
Only the head admin can add admins or gate workers, ban an account or read the access log.
Point changes leave a record
A staff member can't correct a balance without writing down why.
Sign-in with a code Built, not live yet
Staff and students will sign in with an email and password, then a one-time code sent by email. Sessions end after 12 hours. Sign-in cookies can't be read by scripts on the page.

Security today

What the app does now. The rest is under "Before a full rollout" below.

  • Roles are set on the server by the school, so no one can pick their own.
  • Only the head admin can add admins or gate workers, ban an account or read the access log.
  • A student earns points once per game, even when several scans of the same QR code arrive at once.
  • Every award, purchase, refund and correction is recorded with who made it and why.
  • Replacing a QR code cancels the old one right away, including printed copies and screenshots.
  • The QR code holds only a random code. It isn't a password, and it doesn't hold a name or a points balance.
  • The app loads no ad code, analytics or scripts from other companies.
  • The new sign-in adds a one-time code sent by email, ends sessions after 12 hours, and keeps sign-in cookies out of reach of scripts on the page. It's built, not live yet.

What we promise your district

  • We never sell student data.
  • We don't use student data for advertising, and we don't build profiles of students.
  • We use student data only to run the rewards program for your school.
  • Your school owns its student data and decides who on staff can see it.
  • We follow FERPA (the US student-records privacy law) and use student records only under your school's direction.
  • We'll sign your district's or state's standard data privacy agreement, such as the National Data Privacy Agreement (NDPA), without changes.
  • When your school asks, we delete a student's data within [[DELETE ON REQUEST: number of days]] days.
  • We delete your school's student data [[RETENTION: when, e.g. a number of days after the season ends]], unless you renew.
  • If student data is ever exposed, we tell your school within [[BREACH NOTICE: number of hours]] hours and explain what happened.
  • We'll answer your district's security questionnaire in writing.

Before a full rollout

Sentinel is new. This is the work left before it's ready for a full school year or a district.

  • Hosting and backups

    Not done yet

    Today the app runs as a demo on a single server. A school rollout needs a proper database, backups and stable, secure (HTTPS) hosting.

  • Keeping and deleting data

    Not done yet

    There isn't a written policy yet for how long student data is kept or when it's deleted. Data handling also needs your school's approval.

  • Sign-in with a code

    Built, not live yet

    Email and password, then a one-time code sent by email. It's built but not turned on yet.

  • Privacy law review

    Not done yet

    The app hasn't had a formal review against FERPA or COPPA (the US law on collecting data from children under 13).

  • Security audit

    Not done yet

    There's no outside security audit yet, such as a SOC 2 (System and Organization Controls 2) report. We can describe how the app protects data, as this page does.

  • Accessibility report

    Not done yet

    There's no formal accessibility report for the app yet. The accessibility statement below covers this website.

  • References and results

    One school

    It has been used at one school so far. We don't have references from other districts or from a full year, and we haven't measured attendance results yet.

When you book a pilot, tell us which of these your district needs before the first game.

Accessibility

What's done on this site, and what isn't done yet.

What we've done on this site

  • Every link, button and form field works with a keyboard. On small screens, the Menu button opens the site links, and the Escape key closes them.
  • A thick outline shows which item has keyboard focus.
  • A skip link at the top of every page jumps straight to the main content.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, the scenes on the home page show finished frames instead of moving, and the small animations on other pages stay off.
  • Text and background colors meet the WCAG 2.1 AA contrast minimum. WCAG is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, the standard most districts ask for.

What isn't done yet

We don't have a formal conformance report yet, for this site or for the app. That report is often called a VPAT (Voluntary Product Accessibility Template) or an Accessibility Conformance Report. The app hasn't been formally checked against WCAG 2.1 AA.
