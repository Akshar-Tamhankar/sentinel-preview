What the app stores

Name
To find the right student.
Email
To sign in.
Student ID number
To match your records.
Membership
A random code, its status and when it expires.
Attendance
Game, time, who checked them in, and by QR code or by hand.
Points history
Every change, who made it and why.
Prizes
What's reserved and what's picked up.
Nothing else
No address, phone number, birth date or grades. The new sign-in will add a password.

In place today

  • The QR code is random. It isn't a password.
  • A replaced code stops working at once, printed copies and screenshots too.
  • Points once per student, per game, even if scans arrive together.
  • Every points change is recorded with who and why.
  • No ads, analytics or outside scripts.
  • No connection to GoFan or any ticketing system.

What we promise

  • We never sell student data.
  • We don't use student data for advertising, and we don't build profiles of students.
  • We use student data only to run the rewards program for your school.
  • Your school owns its student data and decides who on staff can see it.
  • We follow FERPA (the US student-records privacy law) and use student records only under your school's direction.
  • We'll sign your district's or state's standard data privacy agreement, such as the National Data Privacy Agreement (NDPA), without changes.
  • When your school asks, we delete a student's data within [[DELETE ON REQUEST: number of days]] days.
  • We delete your school's student data [[RETENTION: when, e.g. a number of days after the season ends]], unless you renew.
  • If student data is ever exposed, we tell your school within [[BREACH NOTICE: number of hours]] hours and explain what happened.
  • We'll answer your district's security questionnaire in writing.

Not finished yet

Sentinel is new. Tell us which of these your district needs before the first game.

  • Hosting and backups Not done

    Runs as a demo on one server. A school needs a proper database, backups and secure (HTTPS) hosting.

  • Keeping and deleting data Not done

    No written policy yet, and your school has to approve how data is handled.

  • Sign-in with a code Built, not live

    Email, password and a one-time code.

  • Privacy law review Not done

    No formal FERPA or COPPA review.

  • Security audit Not done

    No outside audit, such as SOC 2.

  • References and results One school

    No references from other districts, no full year, and no measured attendance results.
