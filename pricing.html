How a pilot goes

  1. You book

    We call you to plan one game.

  2. The week before

    We set up your school. You pick prizes and a few gate volunteers. Students sign up.

  3. Game night

    Volunteers scan. Students get points.

  4. The Monday after

    You get the report, students start reserving prizes, and you decide on a season.

Questions about cost

Who pays for prizes?
Prizes aren't included in the price. Your school, booster club or sponsors choose and supply them.
Can boosters pay?
Yes. A booster club or a sponsor can pay for a plan instead of the school.

Does the pilot renew?
Nothing continues or renews unless you choose a plan.
Does our district approve it first?
Many do. For schools is written for that review.
