What a pilot includes

One game at one school, from the call to the report.

  1. When you book

    A call

    We
    Call you to plan the game.
    You
    Pick the game, and tell us which items in before a full rollout your district needs first.

  2. The week before

    Setup

    We
    Set up your school and your reward shop.
    You
    Choose a short list of prizes and a few gate volunteers. Your office activates student memberships.
    Students
    Sign up and print their QR codes.

  3. Game night

    The gate

    Gate workers
    Check tickets as usual, then scan QR codes on their phones.
    Students
    Get points as they walk in.

  4. The Monday after

    The report

    We
    Send the attendance and points report.
    Students
    Can start reserving prizes. Your office hands them out.

  5. Then

    You decide

    You
    Choose whether to run it for the season.

Questions about cost

Anything marked "not set yet" hasn't been decided.

Who pays for the prizes?

See the "Prizes" note under the plans above. Either way, your school picks the prizes, sets how many points each one costs and decides how many are in stock.

Prizes can be small. The samples on this site are a $5 concession voucher, a $10 gift card and a spirit T-shirt.

Can our booster club pay instead of the school?

Yes. A booster club or a sponsor can pay for a plan instead of the school. Tell us who will pay when you book. How to pay: [[PAYMENT TERMS: e.g. invoice or purchase order]]

Booster clubs and sponsors can also supply prizes.

Is the pilot free?

The pilot price is on the Pilot plan above.

What happens after the pilot?

You decide whether to run it for the season. Nothing continues or renews unless you choose a plan.

How do we cancel?

[[CANCEL TERMS: how to cancel, notice needed, and any refund]]

What happens to your students' data when you stop is on the for schools page.

Do we need to buy any hardware?

No. Gate workers use the camera on their own phones, in a web browser. There's no app to install.

Does our district have to approve it first?

Many districts review an app before students use it. The for schools page is written for that review and prints cleanly. Tell us on the call what your district asks for.

Can we pilot at a sport other than football?

Yes. Your office creates the game in the app, so pick the game that suits you.
