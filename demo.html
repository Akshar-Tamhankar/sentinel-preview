About the demo

Nothing here reaches a real school or student.

  • Sample data onlyThe students, games, prizes and points are made up. Names like Ava Clark and Jordan Brooks are samples.
  • Nothing goes to a serverThe demo runs fully in your browser and keeps its sample data there. Nothing you do in it is sent to a server.
  • Reset any timeThe Reset demo button in the orange bar puts all the sample data back the way it started.
